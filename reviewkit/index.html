Questions
FAQ
What happens with a really bad review?
Anything 1 or 2 stars, or written in an angry/serious tone regardless of star rating, is held for you instead of posted automatically. You'll see it in your dashboard and can approve, edit, or respond yourself.
Will it sound like a robot?
No -- every reply references the specific thing the reviewer said, in the tone you set during onboarding (friendly, professional, or warm). It's checked by a second AI pass before anything posts to make sure it reads like a real reply, not boilerplate.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes -- month-to-month, cancel anytime from your account, no contract.
Do I need to be technical to set this up?
No -- connecting your Google Business Profile is a standard one-click login flow, and the tone quiz is five multiple-choice questions. No setup call needed.
