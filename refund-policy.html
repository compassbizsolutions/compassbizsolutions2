Refund Policy
Effective date: April 21, 2025  ·  compassbizsolutions.com

Short version: Digital products can be refunded within 7 days of purchase if you haven't used the product. If something went wrong on our end, we'll make it right.

Digital Products

All products sold by Compass Business Solutions are digital goods delivered electronically. Because of the nature of digital products, our refund policy is as follows:

Profit Leak Snapshot ($99)

We offer a full refund within 7 days of purchase if:

  • You have not yet submitted your intake answers and received your diagnostic report, OR
  • There was a technical error on our part that prevented delivery of your snapshot

Once your diagnostic report has been generated and delivered, the Snapshot is considered used and is non-refundable. This is because the core deliverable — your personalized profit leak analysis — has been provided.

FixKit 30-Day Plan ($299) and Bundle ($599)

We offer a full refund within 7 days of purchase if:

  • You have not yet accessed the FixKit portal or begun your intake, OR
  • There was a technical failure preventing you from accessing the product

After 7 days, or once you have accessed your plan and begun the program, FixKit plans are non-refundable. The program content, templates, and daily guidance are delivered and accessible upon purchase.

If you purchased a FixKit plan after upgrading from a Snapshot, the 7-day window begins at the time of the FixKit purchase, not the Snapshot purchase.

Document Templates

Individual document templates are non-refundable once downloaded. If a template was not delivered or was defective, contact us within 7 days and we will re-deliver or issue a credit.

Exceptions — We Will Always Refund

Regardless of the above, we will issue a full refund in the following situations:

  • Duplicate charges — if you were charged more than once for the same product
  • Technical failure — if a verified technical error on our end prevented access to your purchase
  • Unauthorized charge — if you have evidence your payment method was used without your authorization
How to Request a Refund

Email support@compassbizsolutions.com with the subject line "Refund Request" and include:

  • Your name and email address used at purchase
  • The product you purchased
  • The date of purchase
  • A brief description of why you're requesting a refund

We will respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed through Paddle and typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank or card issuer.

Consulting Services

Refund terms for custom consulting engagements are specified in the individual service agreement. If no agreement specifies otherwise, a 50% refund is available if cancelled before work begins; no refund after work has commenced.
