About
Jen Voiselle
Founder, Compass Business Solutions

For 25 years I worked in business and technology process analysis across three major organizations — finding exactly where time and money disappear, how the individual parts of a business run, and how the pieces fit together. Automating the stuff that shouldn't be manual. Fixing the gaps that were quietly costing time, efficiency, and money.

That whole time I watched friends in the trades — and small business owners across industries — working themselves into the ground with the same operational problems I'd spent my career solving. They just never had anyone with that background in the room.

I live in Mashpee and work with trades businesses here on Cape Cod and beyond. When you book a call, you talk to me.

Compass is what happens when 25 years of corporate-level process work gets pointed at the guys who actually need it — not another company that already has ten people doing this.
