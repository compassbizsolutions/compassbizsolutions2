Note on Credit Card Processing Fees
Some states — including Massachusetts, Connecticut, and Maine — prohibit charging customers a separate fee for paying by credit card. The legal workaround is the "Bake Processing Cost Into Estimates" option below — it increases your estimated price slightly to cover processing costs without ever mentioning credit cards. No line item, no label, fully compliant.
