Questions

Will raising prices cost me customers?

Rarely, when it's done well. What customers react to is a surprise. Early notice, a clear reason and no apology keep almost everyone. Existing quotes keep their price, and maintenance contracts move at renewal.

Do you send the price notices, or do I?

Your choice. Send me your customer list and I'll send them in your business's name, or I'll hand you finished files ready to send yourself.

Is the invoice catch-up a collection agency?

No. It's friendly reminders sent in your name, the same follow-up a good office manager would do. Customers pay you directly. No threats, no collection fees, and nothing reported anywhere. If an invoice needs more than reminders, I flag it for you.

What software do you work with?

Anything. QuickBooks, Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, a spreadsheet or a stack of paper invoices.

Who's doing the work?

Me, Jen Voiselle. I spent 23 years building the systems that keep businesses running, and I started Compass to do the same for trades and home service owners.
